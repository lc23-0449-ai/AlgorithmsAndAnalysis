One algorithm performs much better on dense arrays
(which are full of random numbers) and the other performs
much better on sparse arrays (where almost all of the elements are zero).
Which is which and how do you explain this behavior?

Brute force works much better on dense arrays whereas fingerprint works much better at sparse arrays
The brute force can immediately rule out large chunks of data that dont match the pattern in large dense arrays
as the many numbers don't match the pattern whereas the fingerprint must search through more of the text before moving
on to the next length of the pattern.
For sparse arrays the opposite works out where the fingerprint can jump larger amounts of space before needing to compare
whereas the brute force can only jump ahead so far at a time so in larger array sizes the pattern length adds up.
